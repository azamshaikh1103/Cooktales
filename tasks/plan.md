# Content Plan: Thanksgiving Grocery List for 8

## Overview

Create one original CookTale story for US Thanksgiving 2026: a practical grocery and prep guide for eight people. It complements the published make-ahead sides story instead of repeating it.

## Content decisions

- Search intent: people planning a manageable Thanksgiving meal and shopping list.
- Reader promise: clear quantities, a short shopping order, and no-waste leftovers guidance.
- Structure: short paragraphs, headings, one divider, checklists, and a closing tip.
- Visuals: one real kitchen-table hero photo and two optional real process photos; no AI-looking food art.

## Task list

### Phase 1: Research and outline

- [ ] Verify food-safety and serving guidance from official sources.
- [ ] Choose an original title, description, search tags, and photo plan.

### Phase 2: Write and format

- [ ] Write short, useful sections in separate CookTale blocks.
- [ ] Add a real cover photo, practical lists, and a clear next step for the reader.

### Phase 3: Review and publish

- [ ] Check that the advice is distinct from the make-ahead-sides story.
- [ ] Review title, cover, section spacing, and links before publication.

## Risks

- Food quantities depend on the menu and guests; label them as a flexible planning guide, not a guarantee.
- Only use photos with permission or a valid stock licence.
